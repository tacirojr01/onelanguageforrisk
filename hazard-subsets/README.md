# Sector hazard subsets

Produced by the method of chapter 24 (open problem 2): for each sector, every hazard of the UNDRR/ISC dictionary [24] with its score, its reliability, the inclusion decision and the reason, derived from three streams of evidence (the organization's own loss history, the sector's published incident data, and the INFORM hazard-and-exposure dimension crossed with the sector's geography [7]).

Sectors, in the order they will be produced: banking; retail and logistics; manufacturing; health; utilities and infrastructure; public administration.

*Status: to be produced; the first subset (banking) is planned for catalog 1.1.*
