# The resolved glossary

Catalog version 1.0 · Appendix A of the book. Every term the framework keeps, with its definition and the retired terms kept as aliases, with the source of each.

| Cluster | Kept term | Definition | Aliases (with source) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acceptable amount | appetite | The amount and kind of risk the governing body wants in pursuit of objectives, stated per category on a scale. | risk appetite (COSO, Orange Book, IRM, NIST, IIA) |
| Acceptable amount | tolerance | The measurable variation around a performance objective that the organization will accept. | tolerance as variation (COSO); tolerance, measurable (NIST); tolerable position (Orange Book, renamed accepted position); tolerance boundary (IRM, renamed risk universe boundary) |
| Acceptable amount | limit | A hard boundary on an exposure, enforced by a system; its breach is an event. | limit (Basel, trading); hard limit, cap |
| Acceptable amount | threshold | The value of an indicator at which escalation is owed. | KRI threshold (Basel PSMOR, NIST); trigger (PMI, EBA management actions) |
| Before and after controls | inherent exposure | Exposure before any control. | inherent (COSO, Orange Book, NIST); gross (IRM, UK practice) |
| Before and after controls | expected exposure | Exposure the controls in place should achieve; a forecast. | residual (COSO, Orange Book, ISO); net (IRM); current (NIST) |
| Before and after controls | measured exposure | Exposure actually observed after the fact. | residual, in registers that measure (NIST detail record: measured) |
| Before and after controls | target exposure | The exposure the organization intends to reach. | target risk (COSO) |
| What happened | event | An occurrence that departs from the expected and may or may not cause harm. | event (ISO Guide 73, COSO, IRM); failure, nonconformity (ASQ) |
| What happened | loss event | An event with a measurable consequence; enters the loss base. | loss event (Basel PSMOR); loss (FSB) |
| What happened | near miss | An event with no consequence this time; enters the loss base with a flag. | near miss (Basel PSMOR, ISO Guide 73) |
| What happened | incident | An event that has been detected, classified and is under response. | incident (FSB FIRE, NIST, Orange Book) |
| What is done about it | response | The decision about a risk: accept, avoid, mitigate, share, transfer, realize, enhance; decided by a named owner. | risk response (COSO, NIST); treatment (ISO); mitigation (one option) |
| What is done about it | control | A measure in place that maintains or modifies risk; carries level, mode and originating response. | control (ISO, COSO, Orange Book); safeguard, countermeasure (NIST SP 800); barrier (ASQ, after an event); control activity (COSO) |
| How bad | consequence | What follows from a scenario, stated against an objective; where several objectives are affected the highest governs. | impact (COSO, PMI, NIST, Orange Book); effect (ASQ); adverse effect (NIST) |
| How bad | severity | The scored magnitude of a consequence on a defined scale. | severity (ASQ FMEA, Basel, INFORM Severity) |
| How often | likelihood | The chance of a scenario, expressed in calibrated words or numbers. | likelihood (ISO, NIST); occurrence (ASQ FMEA) |
| How often | probability | A likelihood expressed as a number between zero and one. | probability (PMI, NASA, risk-engineering.org) |
| How often | frequency | A likelihood expressed as a count per period. | frequency (Basel operational risk) |
| The thing itself | risk | The effect of uncertainty on objectives, recorded as a scenario: condition, departure, asset, consequence. | risk (ISO); risk scenario (NASA, NIST 8286A) |
| The thing itself | hazard | A source of potential harm; coded where a dictionary exists. | hazard (UNDRR HIPs, INFORM, NIOSH); source of risk (ISO) |
| The thing itself | threat | A hazard with intent behind it. | threat (NIST, CISA, FSB) |
| The thing itself | exposure | The value, people or operations in the way of a hazard. | exposure (UNDRR, INFORM, Basel) |
| The thing itself | vulnerability | The susceptibility of what is exposed. | vulnerability (UNDRR, INFORM, NIST, FSB) |
| The structure | taxonomy | The whole hierarchy of categories, organized by source at the first level. | risk breakdown structure (PMI); categories (Orange Book); core (NIST CSF) |
| The structure | category | One node of the taxonomy at any level. | category, subcategory (NIST CSF); class, type (UNDRR); domain |
| The person | owner | The single named person accountable for a risk, control or objective. | risk owner (ISO, COSO, NIST); accountable (IIA); sponsor, champion (PMI) |
| The person | responsible | Whoever performs the work; may be several people. | responsible (RACI usage) |
| The proof | assurance | An opinion given by a party independent of the thing opined on. | assurance (IIA, Orange Book); independent assurance |
| The proof | assessment | An evaluation that may be done by the owner. | risk assessment (ISO, NIST SP 800-30); self-assessment (Basel PSMOR) |
| The proof | testing | Exercise of a control or model against known cases. | control testing (IIA); backtesting (Basel); exercise (Ready.gov) |
| The proof | validation | Confirmation that a model or method is fit for purpose. | model validation (Basel); validation against closed form (risk-engineering.org) |
| The proof | verification | Confirmation that something was done as specified. | verification (ASQ); confirmation of action plans (IIA 15.2) |
| The record | register | The governed list of risks with their fields, in the open schema. | risk register (NIST IR 8286, ISO, PMI); risk log (PMI, older usage) |
| The record | detail record | The full record behind a register row. | Risk Detail Record (NIST IR 8286) |
| The record | profile | A summary of a register against outcomes or tiers; current and target. | profile (NIST CSF 2.0, AI RMF); enterprise risk profile (NIST 8286C) |
| The record | log | A time-ordered record of events or actions. | incident log, decision log, loss database (Basel) |
| The plan | scenario | A described future state with its drivers. | scenario (NGFS, EBA, NASA, NIST 8286A, WEF) |
| The plan | stress test | The projection of the organization's position under an adverse scenario. | stress test (EBA, Basel) |
| The plan | reverse stress test | The search for the scenario that breaks a stated objective. | reverse stress test (EBA) |
| The plan | simulation | The method by which a distribution of outcomes is produced. | Monte Carlo (risk-engineering.org, PMI, NASA) |
| Governance | governing body | The board or equivalent to which management is accountable. | board (COSO, Orange Book); governing body (IIA); accountable executive |
| Governance | three lines | Management, oversight functions, internal audit, each with defined roles. | three lines (IIA); first, second, third line |
| Governance | culture | The shared attitudes and behaviors toward risk, measured in aspects. | risk culture (IRM, COSO, Orange Book) |
| Objectives | objective | A stated aim against which risk and consequence are measured, with a tolerance. | objective (COSO, ISO, NASA); performance measure (NASA RIDM); outcome (NIST CSF) |
| Objectives | tier | The level of the organization a risk is recorded at: enterprise, organization or process, system. | tier (NIST SP 800-39, IR 8286); level (IRM: strategic, tactical, operational) |
| Indicators | indicator | A measured quantity with a threshold; leading if it anticipates, lagging if it records. | key risk indicator (Basel PSMOR, NIST); early-warning indicator (Basel liquidity); metric |
| Indicators | trend | The direction of an indicator or score over time. | trend (Orange Book, INFORM, WEF) |
| Continuity | business impact analysis | The assessment of impact over time per process, giving tolerable outage and recovery targets. | BIA (Ready.gov, NIST 8286D, ISO 22301) |
| Continuity | recovery time objective | How quickly a function or resource must be restored. | RTO (Ready.gov, ISO 22301) |
| Continuity | recovery point objective | How much data loss is tolerable, which dictates backup frequency. | RPO (Ready.gov) |
| Maturity | maturity | The degree to which practices are defined, repeated and improved, expressed as a tier or profile. | tiers (NIST CSF, as the framework itself cautions); maturity model (COSO 2026, IIA) |
| Third parties | third party | An external provider of a service, product or component. | vendor, supplier (CISA); service provider (FSB); third party (IIA) |
| Third parties | nth party | A provider to a provider; visible through bills of materials. | nth party (FSB, CISA) |
| Third parties | component | A unit of software or hardware within a product, identified in a bill of materials. | component (CISA SBOM, HBOM) |
| Opportunity | opportunity | A risk with a favorable consequence; a first-class register entry with the responses realize, share, enhance. | opportunity (ISO, COSO, IRM, NIST IR 8286, NASA, NIST CSF GV.RM-07) |
| Decision | decision record | The documented choice among alternatives with rationale, owner and date. | selection report (NASA RIDM); plan of action and milestones (NIST); minute |
| Decision | acceptance above appetite | A decision to run a risk whose measured exposure exceeds the appetite position; communicated to the governing body. | acceptance above appetite (IIA 11.5) |
| Aggregation | composite index | A score built from indicators by normalization and aggregation, published with its reliability. | index (INFORM); score; rating |
| Aggregation | portfolio view | Risk looked at across objectives or entities rather than one risk at a time. | portfolio view (COSO); aggregated risk (NASA); enterprise aggregation (NIST 8286C) |
| Method | technique | A declared method for identification or analysis, from a recognized catalog. | technique (IEC 31010); tool (ASQ); method |
| Method | criteria | The written rules, set before assessment, for judging likelihood, consequence and acceptability. | risk criteria (ISO 31000); scales (PMI); risk model (NIST SP 800-30) |
| Method | calibration | The confrontation of estimates with outcomes and the adjustment that follows. | calibration (PMI library); backtesting (Basel) |
| Method | precision | How sure an estimate is, recorded with it. | precision (PMI); confidence; uncertainty (NASA) |
| Currency | version | The identified edition of a source, scenario or input, with its retraction status. | version, vintage (NGFS); revision (NIST); edition (ISO, COSO) |
